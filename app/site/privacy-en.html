Privacy Policy

Effective: November 12, 2026 · This is an English translation of the Korean privacy policy. If they differ, the Korean version applies.

The wishkit team ("we") protects users' personal information under the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and sets out this policy so that related concerns can be handled quickly.

Notes and tag photos are visible only to you. Friends see only products in folders you set to "public", without notes. However, if you share a 살까말까 by link, the notes on the selected products are visible to anyone with the link (see section 2).

2. Retention and deletion

  • Your account information and content are deleted immediately when you delete your account. This includes posts you sent to other users, comments and votes you left on others' posts, and comment threads you created.
  • A 살까말까 shared by link is a web page that anyone with the link can view without signing in, and it is deleted automatically 28 days after it is created. It contains the sender's name, the selected products, the post note and the notes written on those products.
  • Report records are kept for 1 year after they are resolved, for dispute handling and abuse prevention, and then deleted.
  • Where the law requires retention, we keep data for that period.
  • Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered.

3. Sharing with third parties

We do not provide your personal information to third parties. Content you choose to send to friends or share by link can be seen by its recipients, and we may disclose information when required by law.

Transfers happen over the network while you use the service. If you do not want them, you can avoid the related features (automatic product reading, allowing ad tracking) or delete your account.

5. Your rights

  • You can view and edit your information in the app at any time (My Page → Settings).
  • You can delete your account and all data immediately at My Page (마이페이지) → Settings (⚙) → Delete account (회원 탈퇴). To request deletion without the app, see Account deletion request.
  • You can also email us to access, correct, delete, or stop processing your information. We respond within 10 days.
  • You can change ad-tracking permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

6. Children under 14

Children under 14 cannot sign up. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 14, we delete it immediately.

7. Security measures

  • Passwords are stored encrypted by Amazon Cognito and are not visible to us.
  • All communication is encrypted with HTTPS, and the server checks permissions so that only you can read your private data.
  • Access to storage is limited to the minimum number of operators.
